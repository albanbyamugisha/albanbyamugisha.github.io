# Project brief

## Purpose and audience

Create a long-term professional portfolio for Byamugisha Alban, with a broad audience including employers, clients, collaborators, and general visitors. Use a general invitation such as “Let’s connect”; do not advertise specific work availability. The owner delegates all website phrasing and visual styling to the developer, while professional claims remain grounded in supplied evidence.

## Confirmed requirements

- Work in the renamed portfolio workspace; host at https://albanbyamugisha.github.io.
- Use a framework. Astro has been selected after comparison with Next.js and Vite-based approaches.
- English-only launch; display location as Uganda.
- Full-name wordmark, no BA monogram. Portrait-based favicon.
- Responsive light/dark theme switch with saved preference; initially follow system preference.
- Futuristic styling, interactive 3D, layered glass surfaces, bento layouts, and coordinated typography/motion.
- Home, About, Projects, Skills & Learning, Insights, and Contact.
- Downloadable CV; public contact details and social links supplied by the owner.
- Contact form delivering to albanbyamugisha@gmail.com through an external service.
- All 25 credentials/recognitions in one location, with previews and links; no topic separation or filtering.
- Implemented projects only, modest factual descriptions, no project screenshots, no invented group contributions.
- Full supplied article on the site and an external LinkedIn link.
- Pages CMS for browser-based content editing, backed by portable GitHub files.
- Detailed documentation covering discovery through maintenance.

## Content boundaries

No gallery page, extra experience section with invented entries, planning-stage projects, percentage skill meters, unsupported proficiency claims, fabricated testimonials, or invented project outcomes. The source CV establishes student status and expected degree completion; an ambitious LinkedIn headline is not evidence of employment or professional accreditation.

## Success criteria

A visitor can understand the owner’s background, inspect projects and credentials, read an article, download the CV, and contact the owner. The owner can update structured content through an authenticated editor, then verify a successful build and publication without changing layout code. The public site remains usable if JavaScript, WebGL, or animation is unavailable.
